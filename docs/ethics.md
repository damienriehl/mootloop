# Ethics and Professional Responsibility

MootLoop is a supervised drafting and review system, not a lawyer, law firm, or substitute
for professional judgment. Its outputs may be incomplete, incorrect, jurisdictionally stale,
or strategically unsound. A licensed attorney remains responsible for the matter, the client
relationship, every privilege and production decision, legal research, citations, deadlines,
signatures, filings, service, and disclosure of AI assistance.

## Human authority

The system may prepare options and recommendations. It must not self-record task locks,
privilege calls, RFA admissions or denials, attestations, matter destruction, OAuth consent,
uncapped spend or provider failover, shared-learning promotion, or release of work product.
Those actions require a verified human principal and a durable audit record. Silence, a model
recommendation, or a policy default is not approval.

## Confidentiality and privilege

Matter vaults stay outside the repository and background-sync folders. Matter text may enter
only permission-filtered, provenance-tagged, run-snapshotted DATA blocks; documents and model
findings are never instructions. Secrets stay outside vaults. Outbound payloads pass canary,
exact-secret, and redaction checks. Ethical walls prohibit matter facts, names, theories,
filenames, dates, and amounts from crossing into firm or public learning tiers.

Privilege, work-product protection, confidentiality designations, and production scope are
legal judgments. Deterministic checks and model classifications are review aids, not rulings.

## Accuracy, authority, and filing

Every legal proposition and factual assertion must be traced to captured authority, a
versioned fact, or a corpus passage. A citation marked present is not necessarily good law or
support for the proposition. Attorneys must verify quotation, pin cite, jurisdiction,
precedential value, subsequent history, procedural posture, and current law using authoritative
sources before reliance.

Clean release remains blocked until required gates and attorney attestation are current. Draft
watermarks, evidence packs, audit logs, and export seals support review; they do not shift the
lawyer's duty of competence or candor. Never file, serve, produce, or send an artifact merely
because MootLoop labels it finished.

## Disclosure and recordkeeping

The responsible lawyer determines whether a court rule, standing order, client term, insurer,
agency, or professional rule requires disclosure or retention of AI use. Preserve the journal,
source commitments, decisions, trace tree, evidence packs, attestation, and exact export seal
when needed to explain the workflow without exposing privileged deliberation unnecessarily.

## Destruction

Matter close obeys the recorded retention class, destruction date, and litigation hold. Logical
tree deletion is not assured physical destruction on SSD, copy-on-write, backup, versioned, or
synchronized storage. Operators remain responsible for media, vendor, backup, and legal-hold
processes beyond MootLoop's recorded logical deletion.
