More than a first draft.

Six personas take different roles in the review. You can inspect the work as it moves through the loop.

  1. Draft and review

    The Associate develops the work. The Partner reviews its support, framing and completeness.

  2. Test the other side

    Opposing-Counsel Associate and Partner challenge the position and expose weaknesses.

  3. Revise and assess

    The drafting team responds. The Judge and Rubric Judge assess the work against the task's criteria.

A human attorney on the loop.

Professional judgment has an explicit place in the workflow. Privilege decisions, admission dispositions and attorney attestation remain human responsibilities.

Make it your own locally.

The public site is a read-only viewer. To replay examples or work with your own materials, install MootLoop on your computer.

Start with Python 3.12 and uv. The local-use guide walks through installation, demo downloads and replay commands.

Read the local-use guide

Get the source

git clone https://github.com/damienriehl/mootloop.git
cd mootloop
uv sync
uv run mootloop --help

Keep matter files outside the repository and background-sync folders. Prepared replays require no provider key; other workflows have their own setup requirements.
